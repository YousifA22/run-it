Using Run It

Run It lets you book spots in 7 v 7 pickup soccer games organised by the match organiser. By creating an account, you agree to use the app only for booking and managing your participation in these games.

Bookings and cancellations

When you book a spot, it's yours until the session starts. You can cancel your spot only when another player is on the waitlist to take it — this protects the game from last-minute no-shows. If you leave the waitlist, you can do so at any time without penalty.

Payment

Some sessions may require payment. When payment is enabled, you'll be charged through your device's payment method (Apple Pay or Google Pay) at the time of booking. Refunds for cancelled sessions are issued at the organiser's discretion.
